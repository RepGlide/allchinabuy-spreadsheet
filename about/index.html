About This Resource

A reading legend and editorial key built around a shared AllChinaBuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use an AllChinaBuy spreadsheet. It offers a four-label reading legend — visible in the sheet, visible after opening, likely to change, and confirm at the destination — so each row is read by source and changeability instead of by assumption.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official AllChinaBuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by AllChinaBuy or Google. It is not a store, a product database, a QC certification, a shopping agent, an order tracker, a fee calculator, or a purchase guarantee.

The reading legend

Everything here follows four labels. Ultramarine marks what is visible in the sheet. Vermilion marks what appears only after opening a link. Moss green marks details likely to change. Soft violet marks facts to confirm at the destination. The colors describe only source and changeability — never whether an item is good, bad, authentic, or verified.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any row.
  4. Re-confirm changeable facts on the actual destination page.

Reading example — not live data, verification, or a saved record.
